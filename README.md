# Kelly's Giraffe Adventure Vol. 6: Cocoa Castle

Kelly won a golden ticket! She and her giraffe tour Cocoa Castle, a wild candy factory, to save Peter and her birthday cake from sneaky Baron Sourpuss. Play on foot as Kelly or ride her giraffe through 10 candy rooms (chocolate river, bubble gum lab, blueberry room, nut sorting, TV room, glass elevator) and beat 5 silly candy bosses.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/kellys-giraffe-adventure-vol-6/

Controls: swipe and hold anywhere to move, swipe up or JUMP to jump. ACTION: on foot punch (double-tap kick); riding: head bonk, neck ram (double-tap), tongue, ring swing, neck grab (hold UP at a ledge) and neck pound (DOWN+ACTION in the air). Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Z/Space jump, X action, C ram, P pause, M sound. Controllers: stick/D-pad move, A jump, X/B action, Y ram, Start pause.

All characters are original.
